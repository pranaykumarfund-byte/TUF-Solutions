## 📝 Problem Statement

You are given two integers **n1** and **n2** . You need find the **Greatest Common Divisor (GCD)** of the two given numbers. Return the **GCD** of the two numbers.

The **Greatest Common Divisor (GCD)** of two integers is the **largest** positive integer that **divides** both of the integers.

### Example 1

<p>

**Input:** n1 = 4, n2 = 6</p><p>

**Output:** 2</p><p>

**Explanation:** Divisors of n1 = 1, 2, 4, Divisors of n2 = 1, 2, 3, 6</p>Greatest Common divisor = 2.

### Example 2

<p>

**Input:** n1 = 9, n2 = 8</p><p>

**Output:** 1</p><p>

**Explanation:** Divisors of n1 = 1, 3, 9 Divisors of n2 = 1, 2, 4, 8.</p>Greatest Common divisor = 1.

### Constraints

- 1 <= n1, n2 <= 1000
