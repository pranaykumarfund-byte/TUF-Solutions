## 📝 Problem Statement

You are given two integers **n1** and **n2** . You need find the **Lowest Common Multiple (LCM)** of the two given numbers. Return the **LCM** of the two numbers.

The **Lowest Common Multiple (LCM)** of two integers is the **lowest** positive integer that is **divisible** by **** both the integers.

### Example 1

<p>

**Input:** n1 = 4, n2 = 6</p><p>

**Output:** 12</p><p>

**Explanation:** 4 * 3 = 12, 6 * 2 = 12.</p>12 is the lowest integer that is divisible both 4 and 6.

### Example 2

<p>

**Input:** n1 = 3, n2 = 5</p><p>

**Output:** 15</p><p>

**Explanation:** 3 * 5 = 15, 5 * 3 = 15.</p>15 is the lowest integer that is divisible both 3 and 5.

### Constraints

- 1 <= n1, n2 <= 1000
